About Kike

Some artists fill a stage; others hold a moment. Kike Estrada does both: he sings, plays, and leads with the care of someone who knows that every celebration has its own pulse.

He moves naturally between secular and Christian music, and between the intimacy of a wedding and the energy of a theater, hotel, or bar. Those who book him remember the warmth, the punctuality, and a fine ear for shaping the repertoire.
