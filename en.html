About Kike

Some artists fill a stage; others hold a moment. Kike Estrada does both: he sings, plays, produces, and teaches with the care of someone who knows music is lived in many places at once.

His path joins the stage, the studio, and teaching. Those who work with him remember the warmth, the punctuality, and a fine ear for shaping the show, the song, or the lesson to what the moment asks for.
